# Public-framework alignment

InfraGuard AI is **informed by**, but does not claim compliance with, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework or NIST's ongoing Trustworthy AI in Critical Infrastructure Profile work.

Official references:

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- NIST Concept Note — AI RMF Profile on Trustworthy AI in Critical Infrastructure: https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/concept-note-ai-rmf-profile-trustworthy-ai-critical-infrastructure
- NSA / international partner guidance on careful adoption of agentic AI services: https://www.nsa.gov/Press-Room/Press-Releases-Statements/Press-Release-View/Article/4475134/nsa-joins-the-asds-acsc-and-others-to-release-guidance-on-agentic-artificial-in/

| InfraGuard capability | Risk-management theme |
|---|---|
| Asset / mission context | Map dependencies and consequences |
| Provenance verification | Measure data/model integrity and traceability |
| Safety envelopes | Manage operational risk independently of model confidence |
| Human override | Preserve meaningful human control |
| Degraded-safe state | Secure and resilient failure behavior |
| Scenario replay | Test, evaluate, verify, and validate under adverse conditions |
| Mission resilience score | Aggregate evidence across detection, containment, recovery, integrity, and safety |

The repository does not represent NIST, NSA, CISA, a critical-infrastructure operator, or the U.S. Government.
